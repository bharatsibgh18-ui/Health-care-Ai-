This Privacy Policy explains how Health Care AI ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Android application. We are committed to respecting your privacy and keeping your health records under your control.

1. Information We Store Locally on Your Device

Most personal and health-related information you enter in Health Care AI is stored locally on your device using an on-device database so you retain full control:

  • Patient Medical Profile: Name, age, gender, blood group, allergies, chronic conditions, current medications, and emergency contact details that you choose to enter.
  • Health & Wellness Logs: Symptom logs, headache records, vital signs (blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate, SpO2, weight, sleep), water intake logs, medicine reminders, daily step counts, and family caregiver profiles.
  • Scanned Documents: Text extracted from medical prescriptions or lab reports (images/PDFs) using on-device Google ML Kit OCR.

2. Permissions & How They Are Used

Health Care AI requests only the permissions needed for features you choose to use:

  • Camera: Used only when you choose to capture a symptom photo or scan a prescription/lab report.
  • Microphone (Record Audio): Used only when you tap voice input to dictate symptoms via Android speech recognition.
  • Location (Fine / Coarse GPS): Used when you open Nearby Hospitals & Clinics or send a Live GPS Emergency SOS message to your chosen emergency contact.
  • Send SMS: Used only when you explicitly trigger an Emergency SOS or Caregiver Alert SMS to a contact you configured.
  • Physical Activity Recognition: Used to count daily walking steps using your device's built-in step sensor.
  • Notifications & Vibration: Used to deliver scheduled medicine and water hydration reminders.
  • Internet & Network Access: Used to generate AI triage guidance for symptoms or report text you submit, and for optional cloud backup if you tap "Backup to Cloud".

3. AI Processing & Optional Cloud Backup

  • Symptom & Report Analysis: When you send a health question or scanned report text for analysis, the submitted text/image is transmitted securely over HTTPS to generate health education and triage guidance. Please avoid including unnecessary personally identifying details when submitting queries.
  • Optional Cloud Backup (Firebase Firestore): If you explicitly choose to tap the Cloud Backup button in your Profile/Health Log, your profile and health logs are backed up to Firebase Firestore so you can restore or preserve them.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to advertisers or data brokers. Data is processed only by essential infrastructure services needed for app functionality (such as Google ML Kit, Google Play Services Location, and Firebase when cloud backup is initiated by you), or when you explicitly choose to share/export a PDF report or APK via apps on your device.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can edit or delete your symptom logs, vitals, medicines, water logs, scanned reports, family profiles, or uninstall the App at any time to remove locally stored data from your device. To request deletion of any cloud-backed data, please contact us at njssatiya@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Health Care AI is intended as an educational and family health tracking tool. Parents or guardians should supervise use by minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for advertising or profiling purposes.

Medical Disclaimer: Health Care AI is an educational health guidance and self-tracking application. It does not provide professional medical diagnosis, prescriptions, or treatment, and is not a substitute for a licensed doctor or emergency services. In a medical emergency, call 112 / 108 or visit the nearest hospital immediately.
